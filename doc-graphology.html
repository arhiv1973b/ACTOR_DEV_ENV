📖 WHAT IS GRAPHOLOGY?

Forensic graphology is the scientific analysis of handwriting and signatures to determine authenticity. Experts examine:

  • Pen pressure patterns
  • Letter formation and slant
  • Spacing and baseline consistency
  • Line quality and fluidity
  • Signature variations
  • Comparison with known authentic samples

Graphological evidence is accepted in courts worldwide as reliable forensic analysis.
