📖 SYSTEMATIC STATE FAILURES

Since 1997, the Republic of Moldova has systematically failed to fulfill its international legal obligations:

❌ FAILURE 1: Prevention of Torture

The state failed to prevent the torture of 1997. No investigation into the detention facility. No prevention of ongoing torture practices.

❌ FAILURE 2: Independent Investigation

No independent investigation was ever conducted. Perpetrators were never identified. No forensic examination of evidence.

❌ FAILURE 3: Prosecution of Perpetrators

Criminal charges were never filed. Perpetrators remain at large and unprosecuted. No accountability mechanism activated.

❌ FAILURE 4: Reparation & Compensation

Victim received no compensation, restitution, or rehabilitation. No acknowledgment of harm. No official apology.

❌ FAILURE 5: Legal Reform

No systemic reforms to prevent future torture. Torture prevention mechanisms remain absent or non-functional.

⚖️ INTERNATIONAL LAW VIOLATIONS

Applicable Legal Instruments Breached

ECHR Article 3: Prohibition on torture (absolute, non-derogable) — Moldova is ECHR party
CAT Article 4: States must make torture a criminal offense
ICCPR Article 5: Prohibition on torture as jus cogens norm
ICCPR Article 9: Right to liberty and security of person

📌 EVIDENCE OF STATE KNOWLEDGE

The state's negligence is not accidental — it is systematic and demonstrates knowledge:

  • Official detention records documenting the incident
  • Medical reports filed in official archives
  • Witness testimony on record
  • International human rights reports citing Moldova
  • Official complaints filed with state authorities

Despite this overwhelming evidence, the state chose inaction, covering up the crime rather than prosecuting it.
