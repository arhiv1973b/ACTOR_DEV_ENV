## 1. The False Dichotomy: UDHR vs. ECHR
Conventional legal algorithms (and institutional defenders) attempt to isolate procedural norms from their historical genesis, arguing that the UDHR is merely a "declaration" while the ECHR is a "treaty with a court." This is a critical systemic error. The separation of the UDHR (as a moral absolute) and the ECHR (as a procedural contract) is an artificially engineered legal chasm designed to provide immunity to state apparatuses.

## 2. The Chronological Architecture of Institutional Impunity

* **1945-1948 — The Imperative Baseline (UDHR):** The Nuremberg and Tokyo tribunals established the absolute standard: crimes against humanity and torture have no statute of limitations. The UDHR was conceived as a document of direct, literal action — an imperative shield guaranteeing inevitable punishment and unconditional restitution.
* **1949 — The Immunity Firewall (ETS nr. 2):** The General Agreement on Privileges and Immunities of the Council of Europe (Ratified by Moldova in 1997, immediately preceding the Macheret case). This created the jurisdictional firewall protecting institutional actors from direct prosecution.
* **1950 — The "London Collusion" & Procedural Bureaucratization (ECHR):** Developed under immense pressure to protect elites from investigations into early complicity with hostile regimes or colonial crimes, the ECHR introduced procedural filters (strict deadlines, exhaustion of domestic remedies). It transformed an absolute right into a bureaucratic procedure. The concept of "just satisfaction" supplanted real criminal lustration and institutional restitution.

## 3. The S-22 Singularity (1998) & The Poisonous Tree
The judicial verdict S-22 (Oct 13, 1998) legally fixed the fact of torture ("a fost torturat") under Art. 185 part 3. 

Under Article 15 of the Convention Against Torture (CAT) and Erga Omnes obligations, the fixation of this fact creates a legal singularity:
1. It instantly pierces the ETS nr. 2 immunity firewall.
2. It overrides the ECHR's engineered procedural immunities (such as the 6-month rule or national statutes of limitation).
3. **The Doctrine of the Poisonous Tree:** Because the foundation (S-22) fixed the use of torture, all subsequent judicial acts—including the 2025 Appeals that utilized procedural forgery to block rehabilitation—are legally null and void. 

**CONCLUSION:** Jus Cogens norms pierce any formal "procedural immunity" inherited from the defective ECHR framework, demanding the automatic annulment of all subsequent fabricated cases without reference to national or European statutes of limitation.
