3. Prosecutor Obstruction

Prosecutors refuse to file charges. Cases are closed without investigation. Legal doors are closed.

4. Judicial Dismissal

Cases are dismissed on technical grounds. Judges refuse to hear the evidence. Appeals are denied.

5. Victim Persecution

The victim is targeted for additional punishment (e.g., asset confiscation). Retaliation against speaking out.

6. Institutional Silence

Official silence maintained. No public acknowledgment. No institutional responsibility.
