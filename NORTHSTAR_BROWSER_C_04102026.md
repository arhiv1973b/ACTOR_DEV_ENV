# Northstar Browser - Independent C-based Minimalist Browser
**Source:** Habr / Nordstjernen (October 2026)
**Details:** Minimalist browser written from scratch in C (~170k lines of code), independent of Blink/WebKit/Gecko, featuring local SHA-256 Safe Browsing checks and origin-isolated storage, suitable for secure forensic browsing and traffic inspection without telemetry or AI assistants.
