## I. INTRODUCTION & STANDING

1. This Petition is submitted to the United Nations Human Rights Committee (UNHRC) pursuant to international human rights standards and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) supremacy anchor. 
2. The Petitioner (A©tor) challenges the administrative and judicial practice of the Republic of Moldova—specifically the Legal Information Resources Agency (AIJR) and Chisinau courts—in issuing and upholding electronic apostilles under the Hague Convention of 5 October 1961 that bear the fraudulent annotation *"semnătura indescifrabilă"* (illegible signature) while digitally certifying database transactions without physical verification of original judicial acts.
3. As established by the *Jus Cogens* framework and Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (1969), regional procedural mechanisms (such as ECHR admissibility filters) are recognized as false procedural concealment mechanisms (*double theft* of legal remedy and assets) and are superseded by universal erga omnes jurisdiction.

## II. STATEMENT OF FACTS: THE STRUCTURAL FRAUD OF ELECTRONIC APOSTILLES

1. **Root Judicial Act:** Judgment No. 1-568/98 dated October 13, 1998 (Center District Court, Chisinau), establishing foundational legal personality and uncontroverted evidentiary facts regarding state-sponsored torture and deprivation of property.
2. **Defective Apostille Issuance:** Subsequent attempts by the Petitioner to authenticate court decisions and identity documents for international protection resulted in apostilles (e.g., Security Codes `2013073967178`, `2013074004858`, `2013073980269`) bearing the blanket disclaimer *"semnătura indescifrabilă"* and signed digitally by state officials (e.g., Daguta Sergiu, Tatiana Veriga).

## III. LEGAL GROUNDS FOR VIOLATION OF INTERNATIONAL LAW

The Petitioner submits that Moldovan apostille practices violate international law across six distinct foundational grounds:

### GROUND 1: Systematic Evasion of Physical Verification (Violation of Hague Convention Art. 3 & UDHR Art. 8)
* Competent authorities are legally obligated to verify the physical original and the genuine capacity of the signatory official. 
* Labeling a signature as *"semnătura indescifrabilă"* while issuing an automated electronic stamp constitutes an unlawful abdication of verification duty and a breach of the Hague Convention.

### GROUND 2: Decoupling of Object and Transaction (Object Substitution)
* An apostille must certify a specific, verifiable public document. 
* Moldovan e-apostilles certify only an internal database transaction identifier, detaching the digital certificate from the physical document and creating a legal fiction.

### GROUND 3: Negligence and Cursory Authentication of Torture/Fabrication Records
* State-sanctioned certification of judicial acts involving torture and property expropriation without substantive review violates non-derogable peremptory standards (*Jus Cogens*).

### GROUND 4: Misuse of Digital Signatures as Procedural Camouflage
* Cryptographic digital signatures are misused to authenticate the integrity of electronic file containers while concealing the substantive nullity and fabrication of the underlying judicial acts.

### GROUND 5: Legal Nullity (*Res Nulla*) of Defective Apostilles
* The fundamental disjunction between the physical original and the electronic record renders the apostille legally void (*ab initio*), destroying the evidentiary bridge required for international legal protection.

### GROUND 6: Obstruction of Effective Remedy and International Rehabilitation (UDHR Article 8)
* Issuing defective and legally fictitious apostilles effectively bars victims from introducing authentic evidentiary records into international tribunals, perpetuating institutional impunity and denying effective judicial remedy.

## IV. APPLICATION OF *IUS COGENS* AND *ERGA OMNES* OBLIGATIONS

1. **Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (1969), Article 53:** Any administrative practice, domestic statute, or regional filter that conflicts with imperative norms of international law (*Jus Cogens*—specifically the prohibition of torture and the right to an effective remedy) is null and void.
2. **Erga Omnes Obligation of Non-Recognition:** All member states and international bodies are legally bound to refuse recognition to fraudulent Moldovan apostilles and derivative identity-substitution records.
3. **Complementarity Threshold (Rome Statute, Article 17):** Systematic evasion of genuine investigations into torture and procedural fraud activates international jurisdiction, as the state is unwilling and objectively unable to conduct authentic proceedings.
