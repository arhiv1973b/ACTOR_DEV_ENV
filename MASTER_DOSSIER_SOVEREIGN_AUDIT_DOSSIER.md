## EXPOSÉ OF INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSIBILITY

1. **The Core Contradiction:**
   On the physical source document, the official's manual signature is undeniably present—executed in blue or violet ink, cutting across the printed name and title. Yet, when processed through the Ministry's apostille mechanism, Graph 2 is fraudulently populated with **`semnătura indescifrabilă`**.
2. **The Mechanism of Fraud:**
   Officials insert false or incomplete registry data, divorcing the apostille certificate from the real human actor who signed the document. They then apply digital seals (*MoldSign*) to wrap this administrative vacuum in a false aura of technological validity.
3. **The Victim's Trap:**
   The citizen is forced to pay state fees for an apostille intended to grant international validity, only to be handed a defective instrument. When presented abroad, foreign authorities verify the registry, find an anonymized blank (`indescifrabilă`), and reject the document as legally compromised. The state pockets the fee, escapes liability, and leaves the individual stripped of their procedural rights.
