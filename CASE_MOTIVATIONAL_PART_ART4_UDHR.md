## Summary of Motivational Principles
1. **Supremacy of International Human Rights Pacts (Art. 4 Constitution of RM)**: Absolute priority of universal human rights standards over domestic administrative or procedural obstructions.
2. **Rejection of ECHR Margin of Appreciation**: Regional discretionary filters are legally void when confronting continuous violations of *Jus Cogens* (torture, denial of legal identity, property expropriation).
3. **Restitutio in Integrum Mandate**: Direct application of UDHR 1948 to enforce unconditional restoration of rights and asset recovery without administrative or jurisdictional obfuscation.
