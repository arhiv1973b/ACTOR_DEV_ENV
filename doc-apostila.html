📖 WHAT IS AN APOSTILA?

An apostila (also "apostille") is an official certification that authenticates the signature and seal of a document. It is issued under the Hague Convention of 5 October 1961 and is recognized internationally.

"Apostila atestă semnătura, nu conținutul" —
The apostila attests to the signature, not the content.

This is a critical distinction: an apostila proves that a signature is authentic. It does NOT prove that the document's content is truthful or that the signatory had authority to sign it.

🔍 SIGNIFICANCE FOR THIS CASE
