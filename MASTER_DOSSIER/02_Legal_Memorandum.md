### I. JURISDICTION AND ADMISSIBILITY (THE PRIMACY OF INTERNATIONAL LAW)
The present communication is submitted under the premise of the absolute supremacy of international peremptory norms (*Jus Cogens*) and treaty obligations over domestic legislation. The State Party cannot rely on domestic statutes of limitation, procedural barriers, or regional advisory opinions to justify the ongoing denial of fundamental rights.

*   **Application of the Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (1969):** Under Article 27 of the VCLT, the State Party “may not invoke the provisions of its internal law as justification for its failure to perform a treaty.” Pursuant to Article 26 (*Pacta sunt servanda*), the State Party is obligated to execute its international commitments in good faith.
*   **Constitutional Override:** Article 4 of the Constitution of the Republic of Moldova explicitly establishes that in the event of discrepancies between national legislation and international treaties on fundamental human rights to which Moldova is a party, international regulations shall take precedence.
*   **Exhaustion of Domestic Remedies:** Domestic remedies have been exhaustively pursued but have proven systematically ineffective. The national judicial framework has repeatedly applied *ultra vires* logic, utilizing domestic procedural rules to shield the State from accountability.

### II. STATEMENT OF FACTS AND CONTINUING VIOLATIONS
The applicant, Alexey Maceret, has been subjected to a continuous legal and administrative limbo stemming from unlawful detention, torture, and failure to provide rehabilitation.

While initial actions occurred in the past (1997), the consequences of the State's failure to rehabilitate represent a "continuing situation" under international law. This is perpetuated by:
1.  **Judicial Denials by Judge Alexandru Negru:** Circumventing the binding force of CSJ Decision No. 1ra-834/09.
2.  **Defective Summons and CITAȚII:** Abusive procedural practices involving manipulated/withheld judicial summons and closed *ex parte* hearings.
3.  **Appellate Submissions to Judge Denis Băbălău:** Formal challenges (*Curtea de Apel Centru*, Dosar nr. 2r-1026/26) exposing institutional collusion and nullity *ab initio* (*fructus venenati*).

### III. VIOLATIONS OF UNIVERSAL NORMS (UDHR)
*   **Violation of UDHR Article 8 (Right to an Effective Remedy):** Systematic denial of effective remedies by national tribunals, substituting substantive justice with procedural dismissals.
*   **Violation of UDHR Article 10 (Right to a Fair and Public Hearing):** Domestic proceedings failed to meet independent and impartial standards via irregular citations (**CITAȚII**) and closed hearings.
*   **Violation of UDHR Article 11 (Presumption of Innocence and Legal Guarantees):** Unresolved legal status and refusal of formal rehabilitation inflicting punitive consequences without lawful justification.

### IV. CONCLUSION AND REMEDIES SOUGHT
The Applicant respectfully requests the Committee to:
1.  Recognize the continuing nature of the violations and the overriding applicability of VCLT Articles 26 and 27.
2.  Declare that the Republic of Moldova is in violation of UDHR Articles 8, 10, and 11.
3.  Direct the State Party to provide immediate, unconditional, and full legal rehabilitation to Alexey Maceret in accordance with Article 4 of its Constitution.
