# System Legal Framework & Axiomatic Matrix: CASE-MACHERET-1997-2026

**Status:** Integrated and Verified  
**Baseline:** UDHR 1948, Jus Cogens, Erga Omnes, UNCAT 1984, VCLT 1969 Art. 53/64.

---

## 1. Separation of Material Law and Procedural Filters

The system strictly prohibits the conflation of material rights with procedural barriers. Analysis of any state act follows a five-tier hierarchy:
1. **Fact & Prohibition:** Existence of the absolute prohibition of torture (*Jus Cogens*).
2. **State Responsibility:** Arises at the moment of breach (1997).
3. **Individual Responsibility:** Criminal qualification of the perpetrator (Inspector Dulcea).
4. **Remedy:** Availability of rehabilitation mechanisms.
5. **Procedural Time-Limits:** Domestic statutes of limitation (e.g., Art. 112 CC).

**System Rule:** Using tier 5 (internal limitation period) to nullify tier 1 (the established fact of torture) and tier 2 (state obligations to eliminate continuing consequences) constitutes an **independent, continuing violation of international law**.

---

## 2. The S-22 Processing Algorithm

Document S-22 (apostilled verdict dated Oct 13, 1998, establishing "a fost torturat") is processed through a strict six-step vector:
* **Step 1:** Established fact of torture (1998 judicial verdict).
* **Step 2:** Qualification of breach (breach of absolute norm *Jus Cogens* / ECHR Art. 3 / CAT Art. 15).
* **Step 3:** Applicable standard (nullity of all legal consequences derived from torture — *fructus venenati*).
* **Step 4:** Continuing consequences (active conviction record of 2006, database blockages).
* **Step 5:** Restitution demand (full rehabilitation and restoration).
* **Step 6 (Terminal):** Analysis of procedural limitations.

**Algorithm Conclusion:** If a national court (e.g., Judge Tudor Stambol in 2025) bypasses Steps 1–5 and jumps directly to Step 6 (invoking time limits), the court commits an act of **concealing torture under the guise of procedural rules**.

---

## 3. Rejection of "Statute of Limitations = Legalization of Torture"

The system establishes the legal axiom:
The expiration of domestic limitation periods means *exclusively* the inability to apply a specific national procedural mechanism in standard mode. It **under no circumstances** means that:
* Torture did not occur.
* The state is relieved of its obligation to eliminate the continuing consequences of such torture (as the 1968 UN Convention on the Non-Applicability of Statutory Limitations to War Crimes and Crimes Against Humanity establishes non-applicability to systemic state atrocities).

---

## 4. Central Procedural Defect Question

The primary line of attack against domestic judicial rulings (including those by judges Stambol, Negru, and Holban) is formulated as:

> **Is a national court of the Republic of Moldova legally competent—when presented with an apostilled judicial verdict establishing torture (S-22)—to block victim rehabilitation solely by citing procedural time-limits, without prior evaluation of the legal significance of the torture itself and without eliminating its continuing consequences?**

**Answer:** Categorically no. Such action is incompatible with state obligations under international public law.
