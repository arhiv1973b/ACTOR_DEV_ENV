## SUBMISSION 1: Special Rapporteur on Torture and Other Cruel, Inhuman or Degrading Treatment or Punishment

**Target Mandate:** Urgent Appeal / Allegation Letter regarding continuing torture and institutional ill-treatment through prolonged non-rehabilitation.  
**State Party:** Republic of Moldova  
**Complainant:** Alexei Macheret (A©tor)  

### Executive Summary:
1. **Established Torture & Judicial Confirmation:** On August 18, 1997, the complainant (Alexei Macheret) was subjected to severe physical torture (the "lastochka" position / severe physical coercion) by Police Inspector Victor Grigore Dulce in Room 509 of the Police Commissariat. This act of state-sponsored torture was formally established and condemned by the Sentință of the Center District Court on October 13, 1998 (Case No. 1-568/98), authenticated by Hague Apostille No. 7H2Q3790FZEI5.
2. **Continuing Violation & Institutional Cruelty:** Despite definitive judicial recognition of torture, the State Party of Moldova has systematically refused to grant legal rehabilitation, maintaining the complainant's criminalized status. For 29 years, the State has subjected the complainant to a continuous economic, professional, and social blockade (Lucrum Cessans) and deprivation of property (ZhSK-39, apt. 64 / Miron Costin 13/3).
3. **Legal Qualification:** Under international human rights law and the jurisprudence of UN human rights bodies, the prolonged refusal to provide redress, coupled with active administrative and judicial obstruction, constitutes a form of continuing cruel, inhuman, and degrading treatment (art. 1 and 16 of the Convention against Torture, read in conjunction with UDHR Arts. 3 and 5).
4. **Specific Request:** We respectfully request the Special Rapporteur to issue an urgent communication to the Government of the Republic of Moldova, demanding immediate explanations regarding the persistent failure to provide comprehensive rehabilitation, restitution in integrum, and cessation of the ongoing economic and legal persecution.
